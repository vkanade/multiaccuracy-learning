\section{Summary}

\begin{frame}{Summary}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Multiaccuracy and multicalibration are \alert{indistinguishability}
          conditions: no $c \in \C$ correlates with the residual $y - p(x)$.
    \item Multicalibration gives (swap) agnostic learning: a computational
          analogue of learning up to irreducible noise.
    \item Multiaccuracy alone is weak: it can hold while $\err(p) = \tfrac12$
          and some $c \in \C$ has error $\tfrac14$.
    \item \alert{Adding global calibration suffices}: multiaccuracy + calibration
          gives strong agnostic learning, via $\operatorname{sign}(2p-1)$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
