\section{Summary}

\begin{frame}{Summary}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \alert{Multigroup fairness}: accuracy in expectation
          (multiaccuracy) or calibration (multicalibration) on every group
          in a rich class $\C$.
    \item Both are \alert{indistinguishability} conditions: no $c \in \C$
          correlates with $y - p(x)$, globally (MA) or on every level set
          of $p$ (MC).
    \item Achieving them \alert{reduces to weak agnostic learning} of $\C$,
          with relabelling that keeps the marginal over $\X$
          \textcolor{muted}{(KK '09)}.
    \item Multicalibration gives \alert{swap agnostic learning}, but is costly.
    \item \alert{Calibrated multiaccuracy} is cheaper and already gives
          agnostic learning; multiaccuracy alone does not
          \textcolor{muted}{(CGKR '25)}.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
